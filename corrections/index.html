Corrections

When an error is found in any study, it is fixed on the study page and listed here, with what was wrong and what the page now says. If you spot something that looks wrong, please get in touch through tristanhotham.com.

2026

  • Sun and Rain: the UK

    The study gave different yearly rainfall for the same town depending on where you looked: York had 649 mm in the ranking of bigger towns, 662 mm in the list of all towns and 674 mm in the map search, because the two town lists used slightly different points for the same town and the search showed a single 1 km square rather than the town figure.

    Each town now has one point and one figure, the mean of the 3 km block around it, in the rankings, the search and the published data, and the page labels the single 1 km square, which it still shows for villages and postcodes, as a separate measure.

  • Rings in the Rock

    The distance from Ilkley to Mogor was given as about 1,200 km. It is about 1,380 km.

    In the ring anatomy model, the note said that with nine rings the outermost ring is longer than the first four rings combined. It is longer than the first three rings combined, not the first four.
